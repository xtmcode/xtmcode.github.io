Privacy Policy — Crazy Time

Last updated: 4 Feb, 2026

Crazy Time (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user data.

1. Data Collection

Crazy Time does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

The App may display on-device information such as:

  • Time and date
  • Battery level
  • Step count

This information is used only for visual display on the watch face and is never stored, uploaded, or shared.

2. Data Sharing

Crazy Time does not share any user data with third parties.

3. Permissions

The App may request device permissions (such as activity recognition or body sensors) solely to display step counts on the watch face. These permissions are used locally on the device and not for tracking or data collection.

4. Internet Use

The App does not send user data over the internet.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App does not target children and does not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
